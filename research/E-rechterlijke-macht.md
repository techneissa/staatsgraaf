# Workstream E: rechterlijke macht en rechtshandhaving

## Scope covered

The judicial organisation and law enforcement: the Hoge Raad der Nederlanden and its parket, the four gerechtshoven, the eleven rechtbanken, the Centrale Raad van Beroep (CRvB), the College van Beroep voor het bedrijfsleven (CBb), the Raad voor de rechtspraak (RvR), the Afdeling bestuursrechtspraak van de Raad van State (ABRvS), the Openbaar Ministerie (OM), the Politie, and the Dienst Justitiele Inrichtingen (DJI). For every instantie the file carries a legal source and a website, and for every named head a position with the current holder and, where published, a dated tenure.

Entity counts: 24 organizations, 27 positions, 34 people, 37 tenures, 49 relations, 72 sources, 10 gaps. Subtype counts: 22 rechterlijke-instantie, 1 agentschap (DJI), 1 uitvoeringsorganisatie (Politie). Branch counts: 22 rechterlijke-macht, 2 regering (Politie, DJI).

Boundary against neighbouring workstreams. The Raad van State as a whole, the Algemene Rekenkamer and the Nationale ombudsman belong to workstream F; only the Afdeling bestuursrechtspraak is modeled here. The RvR and its leden are in scope because the RvR is the overkoepelend bestuur of the gerechten. DJI overlaps with workstream B, which already lists DJI as an agentschap of JenV; this file reuses the same org id and adds the DG start date that B recorded as a gap. The Politie and the OM are not covered by any other workstream. Defensie-related bodies are out of scope.

The correct name of the CRvB head is president (not voorzitter); the head of the CBb is also president. The head of the ABRvS is voorzitter, appointed by koninklijk besluit under Wet op de Raad van State art. 30a, not president.

## Decisions

- **Ids and slugs follow the entity name.** `org:hoge-raad`, `org:gerechtshof-s-hertogenbosch`, `org:rechtbank-zeeland-west-brabant`, `org:centrale-raad-van-beroep`, `org:college-van-beroep-voor-het-bedrijfsleven`, `org:raad-voor-de-rechtspraak`, `org:afdeling-bestuursrechtspraak-raad-van-state`, `org:openbaar-ministerie`, `org:politie`, `org:dienst-justitiele-inrichtingen`. The DJI id and the DG position id reuse workstream B's ids so the merge is clean. The alternative was a new id, which would have created a duplicate body at merge.
- **Parket bij de Hoge Raad is its own organization.** The Hoge Raad organisation page calls the parket one of three selfstandige onderdelen, and the parket is explicitly not part of the OM. The alternative was to hang the PG position directly on the Hoge Raad, which would hide the independence.
- **ABRvS is modeled under subtype `rechterlijke-instantie`, but the Raad van State itself is left to workstream F.** The relation `part_of` points at `org:raad-van-state`, which F defines. The alternative was to create the Raad van State here, which would collide with F.
- **Branch choice.** Courts and the OM get `branch: rechterlijke-macht`; Politie and DJI get `branch: regering` because they are executive organs under JenV. The alternative was `zelfstandig` for the OM, but the Wet RO places it in the rechterlijke organisatie.
- **Head titles.** president for the Hoge Raad, gerechtshoven, rechtbanken, CRvB and CBb; voorzitter for the RvR and the ABRvS. The ABRvS legal basis is Wet op de Raad van State art. 30a (voorzitter benoemd bij KB), not the Wet RO.
- **Member positions use `type: commission`.** The rechterlijk and niet-rechterlijk leden of the RvR and the PG-leden of the CvPG are seats, not heads, so they are `commission` with `seatsCount` 2, 3 and 5. The alternative was `dept_head`, which would have implied each member heads the body.
- **Date convention.** Where a source states only a month and year (the RvR-leden, Naves as rechterlijk lid), the tenure start is the first of that month with `confidence: medium` and the uncertainty is named as a gap. Where only a year was published, no tenure was invented. Predecessor tenures are created only where a source states both the start and the end (Avedissian, Simons).
- **Acting tenures.** Two waarnemend periods are modeled with `acting: true`: Judith Boeree as waarnemend president CRvB from 1 april 2025 to 31 december 2025, and Eddie Bongers as waarnemend president van het gerechtshof Arnhem-Leeuwarden from 15 maart 2024 to 31 augustus 2024. The alternative was to fold them into the substantive tenure, which would lose the acting flag the reference surfaces.
- **Relations.** `dept_head` from each head position to its body, `office` from the member positions to the RvR and the OM, `part_of` from the parket, the ABRvS, the Politie, the OM and DJI to their parent, and `oversees` from the RvR to the seventeen gerechten it governs. The alternative for the last was to leave the RvR-to-gerecht link implicit, but the RvR page states the toezicht explicitly.

## Open questions

- Should the regionale en landelijke eenheden of the Politie and the parketten of the OM become separate organization nodes under `org:politie` and `org:openbaar-ministerie`, or stay out of scope? Best answered by the orchestrator with workstream B, which already models rijksdiensten.
- Which BWB-id carries the BES rechterlijke organisatie, and should the Hoge Raad's Caribische cassatietaak get a separate legal basis or an extra `sources` entry on `org:hoge-raad`? Workstream H owns the article-level legal mapping and can settle it.
- The Hoge Raad also carries a parket that is not part of the OM; workstream H should confirm whether the wet art. 111-123 reference is the right single legal source for that parket.
- Portraits for the officeholders: hogeraad.nl serves a portrait of Dineke de Groot and Bleichrodt, but the license is not stated on the page. Workstream O must determine reuse before any media entry ships.
- Predecessor chains for the rechtbanken and hoven (for example Moors succeeded by Van Merwijk per 1 oktober 2026, and the Noord-Nederland line in 2024) are not fully built here. Workstream I owns the full tenure chains and can extend them.
