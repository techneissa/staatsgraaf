# Workstream C: ZBO's en RWT's

## Scope covered

The full register of zelfstandige bestuursorganen (zbo's) and rechtspersonen met een wettelijke taak (rwt's), with wettelijke taak, parent ministry, legal basis (instellingswet) and board or head positions where the source publishes them.

Covered:

- **179 zbo's**, exactly the number of rows in the ROO zbo-register listing and its category export. This is 127 `Afzonderlijke zbo`, 47 `Cluster van zbo's` and 5 rows with an empty `Soort zbo` (six CBS sub-units that the register itself lists under Zelfstandige bestuursorganen plus Stichting Milieukeur).
- **66 rwt bodies** that are not already in the zbo register, out of the 135 entries in the Algemene Rekenkamer rwt overview (115 bodies and 20 clusters). The 69 rwt entries that are also zbo's are one organization with `subtype: zbo`, not a second record.
- **80 positions** and **127 people** with **127 tenures**, across 37 zbo's and 1 rwt where the register publishes a governance role together with a start date. Functions are Voorzitter, Bestuursvoorzitter, Bestuurslid, Vicevoorzitter, Directeur(-bestuurder), Secretaris(-directeur), President, Raadslid, Commissielid, lid Raad van Toezicht and similar board and head roles.
- **245 `oversees` relations**, one from each body's parent ministerie to the body.

Boundary against neighbouring workstreams: agentschappen, inspecties and rijksdiensten without their own legal personality are workstream B; toezichthouders with a zbo status (ACM, AP, ANVS, NZa, Kansspelautoriteit and so on) are modelled here because the zbo register is their primary source. The Politie and the OM are workstream E's primary entities; the Rekenkamer rwt list also names the Nationale Politie and this file carries `org:rwt-nationale-politie`, which the merge gate must reconcile with workstream E. Decentrale overheden (G) are out of scope.

## Decisions

1. **The register is the ROO zbo-register, not a separate BZK list.** It lives at `organisaties.overheid.nl/Zelfstandige_bestuursorganen/` under KOOP for BZK and exports daily as `exportOO_zelfstandige_bestuursorganen.xml`. The count that must match is 179. The previous run's naive XML parse found 176; the category CSV and the listing both give 179, so 179 is authoritative.
2. **One organization per body.** A body that is both zbo and rwt is one organization with `subtype: zbo` (the stronger status, and the register is the more specific source). The rwt designation stays visible through the Rekenkamer source. Alternative not taken: two records per body, one per classification, which would duplicate slugs.
3. **Slugs are prefixed by subtype** (`zbo-uwv` style is not used; the official name is used, for example `zbo-uitvoeringsinstituut-werknemersverzekeringen` and `rwt-prorail-bv`). This follows docs/03 section 9's type-prefix rule while keeping official names, and matches no existing workstream A or B slug.
4. **Parent is the ministerie from the register.** `parent` and `partOf` both point at the workstream A ministerie id. The portfolio name `Klimaat en Groene Groei` (1 row, Nederlandse Emissieautoriteit) has no ministerie of its own and is mapped to Economische Zaken en Klimaat; the other ten ministry labels map directly.
5. **Legal source selection.** `legalSource` is the first specific wettelijke grondslag that is not a generic classification. WNT, Woo, Ambtenarenwet 2017, Archiefwet, CAO Rijk, ABP and Pensioenfonds classifications are skipped, because those are not an instellingswet. Alternative not taken: taking the first wetten.overheid.nl link, which would have labelled several bodies with the WNT.
6. **Wettelijke taak has no schema field.** `description` holds the register's `Organisatiebeschrijving/doel` followed by `Wettelijke taak: <Taken & bevoegdheden>`. A dedicated field is proposed as a gap; the alternative, dropping the statutory task, would lose the fact the scope asks for.
7. **Positions are governance roles only.** Woo-contactpersonen (99) and supporting staff (managers of clusters, slotcoördinatoren, juridisch adviseurs, directie-assistenten) are excluded because they are not board or head positions and are not a public function under the docs/03 section 8 privacy scope. Alternative not taken: include every published function holder.
8. **Position type.** A role that chairs or heads the body (`Voorzitter`, `Bestuursvoorzitter`, `President`, `Directeur(-bestuurder)`, `Directeur-Generaal`, `Bestuurder`, `Vicepresident`) is `dept_head`; every other board or member role (`Bestuurslid`, `Lid`, `Vicevoorzitter`, `Secretaris`, `Penningmeester`, `Raadslid`, `Commissielid`, leden en voorzitters van een Raad van Toezicht) is `commission`. A Raad van Toezicht chair is deliberately `commission`, not `dept_head`.
9. **Tenures only where a start date is published.** The register publishes a startDatum for some function holders only; a tenure without a start cannot be stored. `entryMode` is `benoemd` (board members are appointed, not elected); `acting` is set where the role title says `(Waarnemend)`. No `eindDatum` is published for these holders, so every tenure has `end: null` and the dataset has no predecessors yet.
10. **rwt matching.** Rekenkamer names are matched to the register by normalized name, abbreviation (the short parenthesis) and a spaceless form, plus a small rename map for bodies renamed between the 2023 list and the 2026 register (Instituut Fysieke Veiligheid to Nederlands Instituut Publieke Veiligheid, Klassebureaus to Klassenbureaus Scheepvaart, Reclasseringsorganisaties, Participatiefonds and Vervangingsfonds). Loodswezen and Stichting Donorgegevens Kunstmatige Bevruchting are explicitly not merged: they are related to but distinct from the register entries with similar names.
11. **Clusters are organizations.** The register lists a cluster as one organisation and says its underlying parts "worden daarom niet in het Register benoemd". Both zbo clusters (47) and rwt cluster entries (18) are therefore modelled as register-level organizations, with `cluster` in `aliases`. Alternative not taken: expand each cluster into unnamed members.
12. **Person identity.** A person is deduplicated by slug across bodies; names are normalized from the register (roepnaam plus surname, honorifics stripped). No person in this file holds two of the recorded tenures, so no cross-organization merge was needed.
13. **FTE is not modelled.** `schema/v1/organization.schema.json` has no per-organization staff field that keeps the register's decimal Dutch formatting, so the fte figures stay in the raw CSV only. Alternative not taken: round 712,2 to an integer in `employeeCount`, which would invent precision.

## Gaps

The JSON `gaps` array has 78 entries. The classes:

- **7 zbo's with no wettelijke grondslag in the register** (the six CBS sub-units `Bedrijfsvoering en strategie`, `CBS communicatie en nieuws`, `Dataservices, research en innovatie`, `Economie, bedrijven en nationale rekeningen`, `IT en CIO`, `Sociaal-economische en ruimtelijke statistieken`, and Stichting Milieukeur). Endpoint: https://organisaties.overheid.nl/Zelfstandige_bestuursorganen/. Resume: read the organisation page or confirm with the parent ministry.
- **62 rwt bodies with no confirmed instellingswet**, each named in its own gap. The Rekenkamer overview gives the body and its ministerie but not its legal task law, and the ROO record has no specific grondslag. Many rwt's are private bodies with a statutory task rather than bodies established by an instellingswet. Endpoint: https://www.rekenkamer.nl/onderwerpen/i/instellingen-op-afstand-van-het-rijk/rechtspersonen-met-een-wettelijke-taak. Resume: the body's own site and wetten.overheid.nl.
- **Board composition not published for most zbo's.** The export publishes governance functions for 83 zbo's, but a dated start for only 37 of them; the other 142 have no position in this file. Endpoint: https://organisaties.overheid.nl/archive/exportOO_zelfstandige_bestuursorganen.xml. Resume: the Staatsalmanak or the body's site.
- **13 rwt-only bodies and clusters have a board or head function in the cached ROO export but no dated start**, so no tenure is stored for them. Endpoint: the ROO export. Resume: the body's site or a Staatscourant appointment KB.
- **The Rekenkamer rwt overview is dated 14 March 2023.** Bodies added or removed since then are not in the list. Endpoint: the Rekenkamer page.
- **3 ROO records typed as zbo are not on the public listing**: Stimuleringsfonds Nederlandse Culturele Mediaproducties, Stichting Vaarbewijs- en marifoonexamens, Referendumcommissie (the full export types 182 records as ZBO, with 31 duplicated type tags, against 179 listing rows). Endpoint: https://organisaties.overheid.nl/archive/exportOO.xml. Resume: confirm with KOOP.
- **Wettelijke taak has no schema field.** Endpoint: docs/04-data-model.md. Resume: add a `legalTask` field per docs/04 section 18.
- **Woo-contactpersonen and supporting staff are excluded by design** (see decision 7); the raw source keeps them.

## Open questions

- **Do the rwt clusters belong in `organizations`?** 18 cluster entries such as `Academische ziekenhuizen` and `Bekostigde instellingen voor het primair onderwijs` are classes, not single legal persons. They are modelled as organizations for consistency with the zbo clusters. The orchestrator should decide whether to keep, demote or expand them. Best placed to answer: the orchestrator, with docs/04 section 18.
- **Cross-workstream duplicates.** `Nationale Politie` (rwt) overlaps workstream E's Politie; `Havenbedrijf Amsterdam` and `Havenbedrijf Rotterdam` (rwt) overlap port bodies that may appear in B or G; `Politieacademie` and `Nederlands Instituut Publieke Veiligheid` may also appear elsewhere. The merge gate resolves these by slug.
- **IFV and COKZ naming.** The 2023 rwt list says `Instituut Fysieke Veiligheid` and `Stichting Centraal Orgaan voor Kwaliteitsaangelegenheden in de Zuivel (COKZ)`; the 2026 register says `Nederlands Instituut Publieke Veiligheid` and `Stichting Controle Orgaan Kwaliteits Zaken`. Both matched on abbreviation or rename, but whether these are the same legal person should be confirmed. Best placed to answer: the owner or the body's site.
- **RWT bodies with a statutory task but no instellingswet.** The 62 named legal gaps may be unfillable in the register sense, because the rwt status follows from the Comptabiliteitswet 2016 art. 1.1 and a separate sector law, not from an instellingswet. Best placed to answer: workstream H, which owns the article-level legal mapping.
- **The three export-only zbo records.** Whether they are current zbo's affects the headline count. Best placed to answer: the orchestrator after a KOOP check.
