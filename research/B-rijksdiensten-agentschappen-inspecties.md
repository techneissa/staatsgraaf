ZBO's, RWT's and adviescolleges are out of scope here and belong to workstreams C and F; that boundary is recorded as a gap.

## Decisions

1. **Subtype is a single value.** The schema allows one subtype per organization. NEa, RDI and NVWA are published with both Agentschap and Inspectie; they are stored as inspectie because their published function is toezicht. Rijkswaterstaat, RVO, DUO, DJI and Justis keep subtype agentschap. Alternative not taken: a second subtype field, which the schema does not have.
2. **Slug convention.** A slug is the lowercase ASCII transliteration of the published Dutch name: rijkswaterstaat, inspectie-leefomgeving-en-transport, belastingdienst. No subtype prefix is added for agentschap, inspectie or uitvoeringsorganisatie. Two entities carry an "agentschap-" prefix because their official name starts that way (aCBG, Agentschap van de Generale Thesaurie). Alternative not taken: a forced subtype prefix on every slug, which would give names such as agentschap-rijkswaterstaat and break the link to the published name.
3. **DSTA not counted as agentschap.** The Financien page calls the Agentschap van de Generale Thesaurie an agentschap, but the register export does not list it among the 30. It is stored as uitvoeringsorganisatie to keep the register count of 30 consistent. Alternative not taken: agentschap, which would make the count 31.
4. **Directoraat-generaal execution bodies as uitvoeringsorganisatie.** Belastingdienst, Douane and Dienst Toeslagen execute policy as directoraten-generaal, not baten-lastenagentschappen. They carry subtype uitvoeringsorganisatie. Alternative not taken: agentschap, which would overstate their financial status.
5. **Rijksbrede diensten included.** DPC, KMP, RVD, ABD, NCG, AIVD, MIVD and the Defensie bijzondere eenheden are rijksdiensten without a dedicated subtype in the schema. Where they are not agentschap or inspectie they carry subtype uitvoeringsorganisatie.
6. **Parent is the ministerie, not the portfolio.** NEa sits under the Klimaat en Groene Groei portfolio but that portfolio has no separate ministerie id, so parent is the ministerie van Economische Zaken en Klimaat. Same for RVB, whose page mentions Volkshuisvesting en Ruimtelijke Ordening, but whose register parent is BZK.
7. **Head positions only with a named holder.** A position exists only where a source names the current holder. Positions use type dept_head and the role title exactly as published.
8. **Tenures only with a sourced start date.** Only the JIO algemeen directeur, Richard Wiersema, has a published start date (15 augustus 2026). The other named holders are recorded as people, and their missing start date is a gap. Alternative not taken: inventing a start from the retrieval date.
9. **No relations.** The parent field encodes the only structural edge in scope. No part_of relation is emitted because it would duplicate parent. Alternative not taken: part_of edges for the shared services, which would need an intermediate DG org outside this scope.
10. **Legal source per entity where the cache supports it.** Agentschappen carry Regeling agentschappen 2024; NFI carries Regeling taken NFI; RCE carries its organisatie- en ondermandaatbesluit; NEa carries the Kaderwet zelfstandige bestuursorganen plus Wet milieubeheer. For entities without a fetched instellingsbesluit the field is omitted, not guessed.
11. **ZBO's, RWT's and adviescolleges left to C and F.** No zbo, rwt, adviescollege or planbureau is stored here, even when it appears on the same ministry page.

## Gaps

The JSON holds 78 gaps. The main classes:

- **Missing start dates.** 36 named heads have a source that names them but no benoemingsdatum. Each is a separate gap with the source URL and the resume instruction to search the Staatscourant or KB at https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/. Workstream I can fill these.
- **Missing head names.** 34 organizations have a known head role but no published current holder on the cached pages, for example Belastingdienst, Douane, Dienst Toeslagen, IBTD, IGJ, NVWA, DICTU, Logius and RDI. Each gap gives the exact source page to re-read.
- **Buitenlandse Zaken publishes no agentschap, inspectie or uitvoeringsorganisatie.** The cached BZ organogram lists only directoraten-generaal, directies and adviescolleges. Resume from https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/ministeries/ministerie-van-buitenlandse-zaken/organisatie/organogram.
- **Organogram not published as text.** The BZK organogram page does not include an "Infographic uitgeschreven als tekst" block in the cache, and the BZ, SZW and OCW organogram pages do not list dienstonderdelen below DG level. The ministry pages at https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/ministeries are the resume point.
- **Inspectie Justitie en Veiligheid.** The site says Chiu Man becomes inspecteur-generaal per 1 oktober 2026, so the current holder on the retrieval date could not be confirmed. Resume from https://www.inspectie-jenv.nl after that date.
- **Staatsalmanak not unlocked.** The earlier run could not reach the Staatsalmanak through the repository SRU (unsupported index), so benoemingsdata come from the ministry pages and the register. Resume from https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/ and add start dates.
- **Specific instellingsbesluit per agentschap.** The register gives the Regeling agentschappen 2024 as the framework, not the per-agentschap instellingsbesluit. Resume from https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0050264 and the wetten search; workstream H owns this.
- **Defensie krijgsmachtonderdelen.** Their subtype defensieonderdeel is outside this workstream and belongs to workstream A. Resume from https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/ministeries/ministerie-van-defensie/organisatie/organogram.
- **ZBO's, RWT's en adviescolleges.** Out of scope, resume via https://organisaties.overheid.nl/ and workstreams C and F.

## Open questions

1. Does the project want a `directoraat-generaal` subtype, or should bodies such as ABD, AIVD, Belastingdienst and NCG keep subtype uitvoeringsorganisatie? Owner or orchestrator decides; a schema change follows docs/04 section 18.
2. Should NEa, RDI and NVWA be modeled once as inspectie, or is a dual role needed so the agentschap relationship is visible? The reference has one type per node, so the current choice is inspectie.
3. For the BZ posten netwerk, is each ambassade an organization in a later phase? That decides whether BZ truly has no B-scope entities. Owner or workstream G.
4. Should the people records created here without a tenure be deduplicated against workstream I, or should B only emit positions and let I create the people? Merge decision.
